# Little Moon v0.3.1

Mac stats at a glance. CPU and GPU temperature and RAM use, shown as three small moons in your menu bar, for under 1% of one CPU core.

## What you get
- **Moons in the menu bar.** Each moon fills and warms from white to red as its chip heats up. Show any of CPU, GPU and RAM.
- **Click for the full readout.** Peak and average temperatures, CPU and GPU load, memory, disk, the top three processes and the thermal state.
- **A desktop panel** that sits on your wallpaper, under your windows. Choose Desktop, Menu bar or Both.
- **Honest numbers.** When a sensor can only be estimated, Little Moon marks it (≈ and a dashed moon). When there is no reading, it says "unavailable" instead of guessing.
- **Your way.** °C or °F, Large or Medium panel, English or 繁體中文. Right-click the moons or the panel for settings.
- **A short first-run tour.** Replay it any time from Show Tour.

## Install
1. Download `LittleMoon-v0.3.1.dmg` below.
2. Open it and drag **Little Moon** into **Applications**.
3. Launch it. Little Moon has no Dock icon; look for the moons in your menu bar.

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) on Apple Silicon. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Good to know
- If you do not see the moons, a full or notched menu bar may be hiding them. Little Moon then keeps the desktop panel on, and the tour tells you where to look.
- Temperature sensors differ by chip. Little Moon uses a per-chip sensor map and falls back to a generic set (marked "estimated") on chips it does not know yet.
